# Provisional 12-week plan

This is a first-pass sequence, not a fixed syllabus. Each week combines a conversation function, short listening practice and script work that supports that function. Checkpoints are drafts to calibrate in a pilot. The full three-session-per-week draft is in the [course system](course-system.md) and [app-ready course data](../course/course-v1.json).

| Week | Practical focus | Speaking and listening practice | Kana / selective kanji |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Greetings, thanks, apologies and polite openings | Recognize a greeting and choose a suitable reply; complete short greeting exchanges | Start hiragana with vowels and phrase-level recognition; read greetings in kana |
| 2 | Introductions | Give a short self-introduction and answer basic personal questions; catch names and simple facts | Build hiragana recognition through names and common introduction phrases; **人**, **名前** as recognition items |
| 3 | Questions, answers and follow-ups | Ask and answer simple questions, then add one relevant follow-up | Continue hiragana; read question-and-answer chunks in kana; recognize **何** in context |
| 4 | Requests and simple transactions | Make a polite request and complete a short café/shop exchange; listen for the requested item | Consolidate hiragana and begin katakana with familiar menu words; recognize **円** with a kana reading |
| 5 | Items, quantities and practical choices | Order or ask for a needed item; confirm a simple quantity or choice | Continue katakana loanwords and useful sound patterns; review numbers in spoken phrases |
| 6 | Preferences and recommendations | Say what you like or need, ask for a recommendation and respond to a suggestion | Read a small set of menu and preference words in kana; kanji only if it directly helps the lesson |
| 7 | Places and directions | Ask where a place is and follow a short direction; listen for place names and turns | Practice katakana place names and common signs; recognize **駅** |
| 8 | Time and plans | Ask when or how long, make a simple plan and confirm the time | Read common time expressions in kana; recognize **時間** with its reading |
| 9 | Simple explanations and stories | Say where you are going or what happened in a short sequence; understand the gist of a brief account | Reuse kana from earlier weeks; add no kanji unless it is useful for the chosen scenario |
| 10 | Problems and clarification | Explain a small problem, ask someone to repeat or slow down, and request help | Practice reading and saying clarification phrases; recognize **入口 / 出口** if relevant to the scenario |
| 11 | Combining functions | Adapt a familiar exchange when a detail changes; use follow-up questions and repair phrases | Mixed kana review using menus, signs and short dialogue snippets; review selected kanji in context |
| 12 | Integrated travel conversation | Complete a multi-part role-play with less scripting and recover from one misunderstanding | Review useful kana and selected travel kanji; no new script burden |

## Provisional milestone checks

- **End of week 2 — introductions:** deliver a 30-second self-introduction and answer three common personal questions. From five slow, clear questions, identify the key information in at least four.
- **End of week 4 — transactions:** complete a 60–90 second predictable café/shop exchange using a polite request and confirming the item. Catch the requested item or choice in four of five short prompts.
- **End of week 6 — preferences:** state a preference, ask for a recommendation and add at least one follow-up. Identify the recommendation or choice in four of five clear replies.
- **End of week 8 — directions and plans:** ask for and confirm a simple direction or meeting time. Extract at least three of four route/time details from a short, clear dialogue.
- **End of week 10 — repair and help:** describe a simple problem, ask for clarification or repetition, and continue the exchange. Recognize the main issue and requested help in two short scenarios.
- **End of week 12 — integrated conversation:** sustain a roughly three-minute familiar travel role-play across at least three functions, ask two follow-ups and use one clarification/repair phrase. After a 45–60 second clear beginner-level dialogue, state its gist and at least four of five key details.

Checkpoints can be done with a partner, tutor or self-recording and a simple rubric. They are not claims of independent fluency and do not require automated speech recognition. Review the time, speed and scoring thresholds after the first pilot.

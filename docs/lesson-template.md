## 1. Hear it first

- Play or read a short, clear exchange.
- Ask: who is speaking, what do they need, and what response would fit?
- Include a short gist check and one or two key-detail checks.

## 2. Useful language

Keep the core phrase set small. Add a kanji form only when it helps with this situation, and always show a reading for unfamiliar kanji.

## 3. Say it together

- Practice the exchange in short turns.
- Notice pronunciation, rhythm and where a phrase is naturally used.
- Provide kana support beside the spoken material; do not make reading a prerequisite for taking a turn.

## 4. Make it yours

- Replace one detail (person, item, place, time or preference).
- Add a follow-up question or a likely alternative reply.
- Use a role card or prompt, then reduce the support on a second attempt.
